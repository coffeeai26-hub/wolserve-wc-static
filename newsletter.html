A new chapter for Wolserve Western Cape

We are delighted to introduce Wolserve WC Homecare and Wolserve WC Consulting—two new service divisions trading under Wolserve Western Cape (Pty) Ltd and joining our established Repairs & Maintenance services. Our website is now live, and we are preparing for the commencement of Homecare services.

Homecare registration update: Wolserve WC Homecare’s DSD registration application is pending. We welcome enquiries about future care needs; service commencement is subject to the required approvals.

We are excited about what lies ahead and grateful to every family, client, colleague and community partner who supports this journey. We look forward to learning, growing and serving together.

With gratitude, Izak Goss & Sr Corine Goss

Honouring those who came before us.

With care for older people at the heart of Wolserve WC Homecare, we are pleased to include this thoughtful tribute in our first issue. It honours the lives, sacrifices, wisdom and guidance of those who have walked the road before us—values that sit naturally alongside our commitment to dignity, choice, compassion and respect.
