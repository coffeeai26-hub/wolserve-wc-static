FOUNDER · OWNER · DIRECTOR

Izak Goss

Izak is the Founder, Owner and Director of Wolserve Western Cape (Pty) Ltd, established in 2021. He has led the company and its repair and maintenance services for years.

As the umbrella company grows, Izak continues to lead cleaning-equipment repairs, maintenance and inspection, home-safety consultation and stone masonry, while providing company oversight across all three service areas.

izak@wolservewc.co.za
DIRECTOR · HOMECARE & CONSULTING

Sr Corine Goss

Sr Corine Goss is a Registered Nurse with a BCur qualification in General, Community and Psychiatric Nursing and Midwifery, together with a specialisation in Intensive Care Nursing Science.

Her clinical background is strengthened by approximately 25 years of senior corporate experience across healthcare, hospitality, facilities and service operations. She has developed healthcare divisions, policies, SOPs, training systems, quality programmes and practical solutions for complex working environments.

As Director of Wolserve WC Homecare and Wolserve WC Consulting, Sr Corine uses that knowledge to help clients remain safe, independent and well cared for in the place they choose—usually their own home—and to help organisations build practical standards and systems that work.

Corine@wolservewc.co.za
