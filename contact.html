Urgent support for existing clients

Wolserve WC is not a public emergency or ambulance service. Existing clients and their families are informed of our internal urgent support process, the contact steps to follow and the support services available, so that we can respond and help arrange appropriate assistance as quickly as possible. For an immediate or life-threatening emergency, contact the appropriate emergency service without delay.
