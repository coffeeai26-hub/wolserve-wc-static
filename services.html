Stone Masonry

Stone masonry and retaining walls, built with care from the first conversation to the finished project.

Stage 1

Site visit & discussion

Izak visits the site to understand the terrain, the stonework required and what you want to achieve.

Stage 2

Design & quotation

Stone type, wall height and drainage are agreed, followed by a clear written quotation.

Stage 3

Building

Foundations are prepared and the stone walls and features are laid by hand with care.

Stage 4

Finishing & hand-over

The site is cleaned, the finished work is inspected with you and any final touches are completed.
